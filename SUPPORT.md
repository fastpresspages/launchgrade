# Support

LaunchGrade is free and maintained in public. Here is the fastest way to get help.

## I found a bug

Open an issue with the **Bug report** template:

<https://github.com/fastpresspages/launchgrade/issues/new/choose>

Include the URL you scanned (if public), the exact command, your Node version, and the output. If the page is private, reduce it to a minimal HTML fixture.

## A check is wrong or unfair

Open a **Check proposal** issue. Explain what the check currently says, what it should say, and why. Include a page where the current result is misleading. Checks that reward good behavior without punishing small legitimate sites are easiest to accept.

## I want a free human audit

Use the form on the site:

<https://fastpresspages.github.io/launchgrade/#audit>

Tell us the URL, what you are launching, and what is stuck. A human replies with a prioritized plan. No account, no payment.

## I want to collaborate, sponsor, or send a deal

Email **meetcode99@gmail.com** with:

- who you are and what you build;
- the concrete ask (collab, sponsorship, tooling, distribution);
- what you can offer in return;
- a link we can verify.

We are open to honest cross-promotion, open-source collaborations, guest audits, and tools that genuinely help people ship. We do not do paid links, fake reviews, engagement pods, or anything that requires inventing numbers.

## I want to contribute

Read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md). Good first contributions: a failing fixture for a check that misses a common page pattern, a clearer fix message, an accessibility improvement to the report, or a docs fix.

## Response times

This is a small project. Issues and emails are usually reviewed within a few days. Security reports follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## No support for

- Hacking or scanning sites you do not own.
- Guaranteed rankings, traffic or revenue.
- Writing deceptive pages that “pass” the checklist.
- Free consulting disguised as a bug report. The audit form is the right place for that.
