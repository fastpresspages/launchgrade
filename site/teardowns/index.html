What repeats across all four

  1. Trust 38 on every single page. A missing privacy policy is the most common fail on fresh launches — and the easiest to fix in an afternoon.
  2. No email capture. HN traffic spikes and vanishes within 48 hours; four launches had no way to keep any of it.
  3. No Open Graph card (2 of 4). Their links get shared on X, Discord and Slack without a preview image.
  4. No llms.txt / agent map. Every page was effectively invisible to AI agents trying to summarize what the product does.
  5. The technical floor is high. Technical 90–98 across the board — builders today ship fast, secure pages. What's missing is the human layer: capture, trust, share.
