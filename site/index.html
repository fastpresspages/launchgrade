The 60-second pre-launch check

You ship. It checks.
You fix the right thing.

No dashboard to learn, no browser to install, no score without a next step. Point it at a URL and read.

  1. 01

    Scan the public page

    Fetches the HTML, headers and a small allowlist of well-known files: robots, sitemap, llms.txt, security.txt, AGENTS.md. No JavaScript execution, no cookies.

    launchgrade your.site
  2. 02

    Get a weighted grade

    Every check returns pass, warn, fail or info. Categories are normalized and weighted, so conversion and technical issues outrank cosmetic ones.

    A+ · 100/100 · launch-ready
  3. 03

    Fix the top three

    The fix plan sorts by impact, not by checklist order. Each item explains the failure in plain language and tells you what to change.

    launchgrade your.site --out reports

What it actually looks at

Seven categories.
Fifty-three honest checks.

Each category carries a weight. Nothing checks for your favorite framework, and nothing rewards fake urgency or keyword stuffing.

↗25%

Conversion

Can a stranger understand the offer and take one action?

  • Email capture and a specific CTA
  • Social proof and risk reversal
  • Low friction, human contact
◈15%

Trust

Would a careful buyer believe you and feel safe?

  • Privacy, terms and about links
  • Real social presence
  • security.txt and current copyright
⌁20%

Technical

Does the page load fast, securely and indexably?

  • HTTPS, status, speed, compression
  • Viewport, canonical, language
  • Mixed content and analytics
◎15%

SEO

Can search engines understand and rank the page?

  • Title, description and headings
  • Sitemap, robots and schema
  • Image alt text and text ratio
▣10%

Social sharing

Will a shared link look credible in the feed?

  • Complete Open Graph card
  • Twitter/X summary card
  • Share image and alt text
☰5%

Accessibility

Can everyone use the page, including keyboard and screen-reader users?

  • Form labels and button names
  • Descriptive links and landmarks
  • Pinch zoom and skip link
✳10%

AI-agent readiness

Can ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and coding agents read and recommend you?

  • llms.txt and explicit crawler access
  • AGENTS.md and Markdown negotiation
  • FAQ, semantic HTML, no-JS copy

Is LaunchGrade really free?

Yes. The CLI, GitHub Action, MCP server, reports and agent skill are open source under Apache-2.0. The human audit request is free too. There is no paid tier, no account and no tracking.

Does this replace Lighthouse?

No. Lighthouse measures browser performance and best practices. LaunchGrade checks the launch surface: offer clarity, capture, trust, share cards, search basics, accessibility signals and AI-agent readiness. They complement each other.

Do you store the URLs I scan?

The CLI runs locally and sends nothing to us. If you submit the audit form, the URL and message are delivered to the FastPress Labs inbox through FormSubmit so a human can reply.

Can I use it in CI?

Yes. Use the composite GitHub Action or run the CLI with --fail-under 80. The job exits with code 2 below your threshold, so a launch page cannot silently regress.

Does it work on private pages?

No. LaunchGrade fetches public URLs without your cookies or session. For a private page, export the HTML and use the library API with buildContextFromHtml, or ask for a human audit.

Why is it called LaunchGrade?

Because “launch” is the moment everything you skipped becomes visible, and a grade is easier to act on than a 60-item checklist. The letter is a summary; the fix plan is the product.

Who is behind it?

FastPress Labs, a small open-source community for people who ship. YouLauncher is its first trial project. LaunchGrade is the first shared tool. No venture funding, no growth team, no fake logos.
